What is Build & Break?

An innovation-driven challenge that pushes teams to solve real-world problems, defend their ideas, and emerge stronger through expert critique.

Build & Break 2.0 is not just a hackathon — it's a full-cycle innovation experience. Teams are given real-world problem statements, asked to ideate and build solutions, then present them to a panel of industry experts who actively challenge every assumption.

The event simulates the pressure of a real product development cycle — from ideation to refinement — giving participants an industry-like experience that goes far beyond a typical college competition.

01

Ideation

Analyze the problem statement, brainstorm creative approaches, and define your solution's core value proposition.

02

Development

Build a working prototype or detailed solution plan. Translate ideas into tangible, demonstrable outcomes.

03

Refinement

Incorporate jury feedback, strengthen weak points, and deliver a polished final pitch that stands up to scrutiny.

Event Structure

Two rounds designed to challenge, refine, and elevate your solution.

Round 1
16 April

Build Phase

Teams receive their problem statement and have the full day to ideate, build, and prepare their initial presentation.

  • Problem Statement Assigned

    Receive your real-world challenge and understand the scope.

  • Brainstorming

    Collaborate as a team to explore approaches and define your solution.

  • Initial Presentation

    Present your solution to the jury for the first round of evaluation.

Enhancement Phase

Armed with expert feedback, teams refine their solutions and deliver a polished final pitch to the jury.

  • Jury Feedback

    Receive detailed critique and actionable insights from industry experts.

  • Solution Refinement

    Strengthen weak points, address gaps, and elevate your solution.

  • Final Pitch

    Deliver your refined solution in a compelling final presentation.

About ISOC Academic Hub

A student-driven initiative fostering innovation, technical excellence, and collaboration.
